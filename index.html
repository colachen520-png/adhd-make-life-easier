What the energy check-in actually does

Low energy shows one unfinished task and asks AI for a 1–2 minute starting step. Steady shows three tasks; high shows five. Your manually chosen priorities still come first. These are adjustable product choices, not a clinical assessment. Skip the check-in to see everything.

How estimates work

New tasks use a clearly marked 15-minute placeholder until you edit it or ask AI. AI estimates each step and adds those times together. They are rough planning aids; waiting time and interruptions may make real tasks take longer.

Data, AI & voice

AI connection

No cloud AI, API keys or text sharing. Local AI output is editable and may be wrong.

Voice typing

Dictate buttons record up to 90 seconds and append locally transcribed words to the selected field. Nothing is submitted automatically. Microphone access is requested only when you start. Audio is processed on this Mac and discarded after transcription.

Your saved data

Entries stay in this browser. Export before switching from the file version to localhost, then import there. Import adds missing entries and keeps existing ones.

Make room for your week

These are planning limits, not every hour you’re awake. Calendar commitments and task estimates both count.

Usual work days

On non-workdays, personal suggestions fit between 09:00 and 21:00. All-day calendar events are shown as context, not counted as 24 hours.

Calendars & reminders

Connect calendars already added to macOS Calendar, including iCloud, Google or Outlook accounts. Choose which ones to read and label them Work or Personal.

macOS asks for calendar access. The app reads only selected calendars after you choose them. It writes task events only when you press Publish to Calendar. Calendar providers may sync those events to your other devices; AI stays on this Mac.

Reminders on your phone

Publish scheduled tasks to a calendar that also syncs to your phone. Each task gets its chosen alert. Calendar notifications and device Focus settings control delivery. This creates calendar alerts, not a Clock alarm.

Publishing updates previously linked tasks. Moving or deleting a task in this app does not silently change Calendar: publish changes again or manage the event in Calendar. Importing .ics repeatedly may duplicate events.

While this app is open

Local browser alerts can remind you about timed tasks while this page is running. They are not reliable when the browser is closed or asleep.

The app itself is still local to this Mac. A phone app or secure device sync is a separate step; localhost on a phone will not open this Mac’s app.
